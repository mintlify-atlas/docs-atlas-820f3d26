Chromex is a Chrome MV3 side-panel extension that puts Codex directly in your browser. Ask questions about the current page, summarize YouTube videos, edit images, use voice input, and automate browser tasks — all without leaving your workflow. A local native bridge keeps your credentials off the extension and routes requests securely to the Codex app-server running on your machine.

## How Chromex works

Chromex splits responsibilities across four layers so your credentials never touch extension storage:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    Load the unpacked extension in Chrome Developer mode. Chromex adds a persistent side panel to every browser window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the local bridge">
    The native messaging host connects Chrome to a local bridge daemon that talks to the Codex app-server running on your machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in through Codex">
    Authentication flows through the local Codex app-server. Your API keys and tokens stay on your machine — never in Chrome extension storage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Open the side panel, pick a profile with `/`, mention tabs with `@`, attach files, and ask anything about what's on your screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>
